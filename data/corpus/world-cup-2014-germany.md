# 2014 World Cup: Germany in Brazil

The 2014 World Cup was hosted by Brazil, the second time the country had staged the tournament after 1950. Germany won the title, its fourth overall and its first as a unified nation, all previous German wins having come as West Germany.

The tournament's most shocking result came in the semi-final, when Germany defeated the hosts Brazil 7–1 in Belo Horizonte on 8 July 2014. Germany led 5–0 within half an hour. The scale of the defeat, on home soil and with the injured Neymar absent, was received as a national humiliation in Brazil and is remembered simply as the "Mineirazo".

Germany met Argentina in the final at the Maracanã in Rio de Janeiro on 13 July 2014. The match was goalless after ninety minutes. In extra time Mario Götze controlled a cross on his chest and volleyed home the only goal, giving Germany a 1–0 win.

The tournament also marked a record for Germany's Miroslav Klose, who scored his sixteenth World Cup goal to become the competition's all-time leading scorer, overtaking Brazil's Ronaldo. Colombia's James Rodríguez finished as the tournament's top scorer with six goals.
