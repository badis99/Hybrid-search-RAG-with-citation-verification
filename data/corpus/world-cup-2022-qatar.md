# 2022 World Cup: Argentina and Messi

The 2022 World Cup was hosted by Qatar, the first World Cup held in the Middle East and in the Arab world. Because of the region's extreme summer heat, it was also the first held in November and December rather than the usual June and July, interrupting the European club season.

Argentina won the tournament, its third world title and its first since 1986. The final against France, played at the Lusail Stadium on 18 December 2022, is widely regarded as one of the greatest in the tournament's history. Argentina led 2–0 through Lionel Messi and Ángel Di María, but Kylian Mbappé scored twice in the space of about ninety seconds to force extra time.

Messi and Mbappé traded further goals in extra time to make it 3–3, with Mbappé completing a hat-trick, the first in a World Cup final since Geoff Hurst in 1966. Argentina won the resulting penalty shoot-out 4–2, with goalkeeper Emiliano Martínez making a decisive save.

The title completed Lionel Messi's career, giving him the one major trophy he had never won. Messi was named the tournament's best player, winning the Golden Ball, while Mbappé finished as top scorer with eight goals, winning the Golden Boot.
