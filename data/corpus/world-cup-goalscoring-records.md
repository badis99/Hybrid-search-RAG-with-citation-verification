# World Cup goalscoring records

The all-time leading goalscorer in the history of the FIFA World Cup finals is Miroslav Klose of Germany, with sixteen goals scored across four tournaments from 2002 to 2014. He overtook Brazil's Ronaldo, who scored fifteen, when he netted in the 2014 semi-final against Brazil.

The record for the most goals in a single tournament belongs to Just Fontaine of France, who scored thirteen goals at the 1958 World Cup in Sweden. This total is widely considered one of the sport's most unbreakable records, as no player since has come close in the modern game.

The award for the top scorer at each tournament is now called the Golden Boot. Notable winners include Gerd Müller of West Germany, who scored ten goals in 1970, and Ronaldo of Brazil, who scored eight in 2002. In 2022 the Golden Boot went to Kylian Mbappé of France with eight goals, despite France losing the final to Argentina.

Only a handful of players have scored a hat-trick in a World Cup final. Geoff Hurst of England did so in 1966, and Kylian Mbappé became the second in 2022, though Mbappé finished on the losing side. Hat-tricks anywhere in the tournament remain rare enough to be notable achievements.
