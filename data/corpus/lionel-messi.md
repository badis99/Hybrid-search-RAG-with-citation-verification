# Lionel Messi

Lionel Messi is an Argentine forward widely regarded as one of the greatest footballers of all time. Born in Rosario in 1987, he moved to Spain as a teenager to join Barcelona, whose youth academy paid for treatment of a growth-hormone deficiency he had as a child.

Messi has won a record eight Ballon d'Or awards, given to the world's best player. At Barcelona he became the club's all-time leading scorer and won multiple UEFA Champions League and Spanish league titles before joining Paris Saint-Germain in 2021 and later Inter Miami in Major League Soccer in 2023.

For much of his career Messi was criticised for not matching his club success with Argentina, having lost the 2014 World Cup final to Germany and several Copa América finals. That changed from 2021, when Argentina won the Copa América, followed by the 2022 World Cup.

At the 2022 World Cup in Qatar, Messi captained Argentina to the title, scored seven goals including two in the final against France, and was named the tournament's best player, winning the Golden Ball. The victory is widely seen as having secured his place in the argument over the greatest player in the sport's history.
