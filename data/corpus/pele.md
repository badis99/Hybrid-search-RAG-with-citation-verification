# Pelé

Edson Arantes do Nascimento, known as Pelé, was a Brazilian forward regarded as one of the greatest footballers of all time. Born in 1940, he is the only player to have won the FIFA World Cup three times, in 1958, 1962, and 1970.

Pelé burst onto the world stage at the 1958 World Cup in Sweden at the age of seventeen, scoring twice in the final as Brazil beat the hosts 5–2. He remains the youngest player to score in a World Cup final. Injury limited his role in the 1962 tournament, which Brazil also won, and he was targeted by heavy fouling at the 1966 edition.

He returned at his peak for the 1970 World Cup in Mexico, forming part of the Brazil team often called the greatest of all time. Pelé scored in the 4–1 final win over Italy and finished the tournament with his third winner's medal.

At club level Pelé spent most of his career with Santos in Brazil before a spell with the New York Cosmos in the North American Soccer League, which helped popularise the sport in the United States. He is often credited with scoring over a thousand career goals. Pelé died in December 2022, shortly after Argentina won the World Cup in Qatar.
